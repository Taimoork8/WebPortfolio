About

A builder at heart

I'm Muhammad Taimoor Khan, a Software Engineer based in Islamabad with 5+ years of experience building mobile apps, web backends, and embedded systems. I specialise in Flutter for cross-platform mobile development, Django/Python for server-side work, and BLE/embedded firmware for IoT hardware.
